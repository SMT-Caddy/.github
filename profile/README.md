## SMT Caddy

Manual SMD assembly tools for PCB prototyping, designed, molded and assembled in the U.S. SMT Caddy is based in Riverside, California.

**MTI manual tape feeder:** holds cut SMD tape on 35 mm DIN rail and is only 2 mm wider than the tape. One base unit covers 8 to 32 mm tape with low-cost extension kits. It is conductive and grounds through the rail, with no motors or electronics.
